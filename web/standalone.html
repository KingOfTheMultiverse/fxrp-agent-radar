Simulated, not on-chain. Every live agent on Coston2 is heavily over-collateralised, so the dangerous end of the curve never appears in real data. This replays the same scoring functions down a degradation path from 's real current state.

How the score works

  • Collateral buffer — headroom above the liquidation threshold, relative to it. 0% means sitting exactly on the line. Vault and pool have different thresholds (120% and 150%), are scored separately, and the weaker one governs.
  • XRP backing surplus — underlying XRP held versus owed to redeemers. Negative means redemption pays out collateral at the default factor instead of XRP: you get paid, but not in the asset you asked for.
  • Weighting — 40% collateral safety, 30% backing, 20% capacity, 10% fee. Safety outranks price deliberately; basis points never compensate for a failed redemption.
  • Any agent outside NORMAL status scores 0 and is never routed to.
